## When it matters, the answer is one tap away.

Aurora is regularly in the path of typhoons, and ASCOT has three campuses spread across the province. The ASCOT DRRMS gives students and staff a single place to check whether an advisory is in effect, what to do and who to call.

The public site opens with the live status of each campus, a hotline button and short steps for typhoons, earthquakes, fires, and floods or landslides. Emergency numbers are grouped by municipality, so each campus sees the ones nearest to it, and every number is tap-to-call.

## What the system covers

- **Facility Hazard Auditor** — records campus buildings and areas and rates their hazards.
- **Incident Reporting** — logs incidents as they happen, tracks their status and documents damage.
- **Disaster Advisories** — typhoon, earthquake and flood warnings from official bulletins.
- **Drill Tracker** — schedules drills on a calendar and files after-activity reports.
- **SMS & Broadcast Alert** — sends emergency texts linked to an incident and tracks their delivery.
- **A phone app** for students and staff to press SOS, report hazards with a photo and location, and receive advisories. It installs straight from the website, with no app store needed.

## Designing for a stressful moment

The interface keeps the urgent things first: on a phone, the call button sits above everything else, and the steps for each hazard are limited to the three that matter most. A calm, dark interface lets the status colours and the red emergency button stand out.
