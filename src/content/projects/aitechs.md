## A community, one post at a time.

As Multimedia Officer of AITECHS — the Association of Information Technology Students — I designed the visuals that announced and documented the year: from the General Orientation key visual, *A Growing Community: Voyaging to the World of IT*, to IT week competitions, greetings and congratulations for board passers.

## Elections that people read

Student elections need clear information fast. Campaign materials for party platforms, candidate cards and profiles, filing of candidacy, timelines and live results were designed as consistent series, so voters could compare candidates and follow the count.

## Merchandise

The work also includes lanyard designs for the School of Information Technology, carrying the program's identity onto something students wear every day.
