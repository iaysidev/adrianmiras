## Designed for a crowd.

Much of my graphic design work happens close to home: barangay fiestas, municipal sports leagues, beach-resort gigs and local celebrations. These posters have to work at a glance on a crowded feed and still carry the date, time and place clearly.

## Events and leagues

For a barangay fiesta, contests and a street party each got their own bold, colourful poster while sharing the same event identity. Sports leagues got schedules, countdowns and player-of-the-game posts built as a series.

## Posters, people and stories

The collection also includes resort anniversary and live-music posters, a film poster, an illustrated birthday banner, partylist campaign materials and a Women's Month carousel that tells the story of Filipino women with archival imagery and a strong purple accent.

## Things people wear

Lanyard and polo shirt designs carry an identity off the screen and onto something people use every day.
